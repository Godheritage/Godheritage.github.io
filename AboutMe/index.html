Ph.D. student · School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen

I'm a Ph.D. student in GenLab at CUHK-Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Zhen Liu, and currently a research intern at Tencent Hunyuan.

I want language-model agents to design physical things that actually work: machines that throw, mechanisms that lift, parts that have to fit and move together. Most of my recent time goes into building the environments for this, where an agent writes a design, runs it in a physics simulator, and repairs it from what it sees.

Before CUHK-SZ I spent seven years at ShanghaiTech University (B.Eng. 2022, M.Eng. 2025). My master's was in the VDI Center with Prof. Lan Xu and Prof. Jingyi Yu, on human motion generation and sign language understanding. Before research I made games, which is still how I tend to think about simulation.

Can It Actually Work? Synthesizing Functional CAD Mechanisms

Wenqian Zhang*, Yangyi Huang*, Zigeng Xu*, Weiyang Liu, Zhen Liu

arXiv preprint, 2026

A coding agent turns a vague request into CAD; FunCAD-Harness converts each functional requirement into a physics test for that specific design and sends the results back. FunCADBench covers 46 mechanism families, and some designs were 3D-printed to check them in the real world.
